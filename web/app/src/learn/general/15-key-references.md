# 15. Key references

A selection of the most important sources, grouped by theme. All other sources are linked inline.

**Flexibility quantification**

- Ulbig & Andersson, [Analyzing operational flexibility of electric power systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.7618), PSCC 2014.
- Hao, Sanandaji, Poolla & Vincent, [A generalized battery model of a collection of thermostatically controlled loads](https://certs.lbl.gov/publications/generalized-battery-model-collection.html), Allerton 2013.
- Zhao, Zhang, Hao & Kalsi, [A geometric approach to aggregate flexibility modeling of thermostatically controlled loads](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.04422), 2016.
- Panda & Tindemans, [EV fleet flexibility representation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.02729), Electric Power Systems Research, 2024.
- Monterde, Alvarez & Valarezo, [Review of non-firm connection agreements](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40518-025-00268-7), Current Sustainable/Renewable Energy Reports, 2025.

**Physics-informed ML**

- Karniadakis et al., [Physics-informed machine learning](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42254-021-00314-5), Nature Reviews Physics, 2021.
- Misyris, Venzke & Chatzivasileiadis, [Physics-informed neural networks for power systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.03737), 2020.
- Wang et al., [Physics-informed neural network for battery state-of-health estimation](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-48779-z), Nature Communications, 2024.
- Di Natale et al., [Physically consistent neural networks for building thermal modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.03212), Applied Energy, 2022.
- Drgoňa et al., [Physics-constrained deep learning of multi-zone building thermal dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.05987), Energy and Buildings, 2021.
- Ceccon et al., [OMLT: Optimization & Machine Learning Toolkit](https://jmlr.org/papers/v23/22-0277.html), JMLR, 2022.

**Optimisation and control**

- Campi, Garatti & Prandini, [Scenario optimization for MPC](https://marco-campi.unibs.it/pdf-pszip/Handbook%20MPC.pdf), Handbook of MPC, 2019.
- Karg & Lucia, [Efficient representation and approximation of MPC laws via deep learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.10644), IEEE Transactions on Cybernetics, 2020.
- Drgoňa et al., [Differentiable predictive control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.11184), 2020–2024.
- Dalal et al., [Safe exploration in continuous action spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.08757), 2018.
- Arroyo, Spiessens & Helsen, [Comparison of optimal control techniques for building energy management](https://doi.org/10.3389/fbuil.2022.849754), Frontiers in Built Environment, 2022.

**Forecasting and battery economics**

- Hong, Pinson, Fan, Zareipour, Troccoli & Hyndman, [Probabilistic energy forecasting: GEFCom2014 and beyond](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijforecast.2016.02.001), International Journal of Forecasting, 2016.
- Gneiting, Balabdaoui & Raftery, [Probabilistic forecasts, calibration and sharpness](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9868.2007.00587.x), Journal of the Royal Statistical Society B, 2007.
- Xu, Zhao, Zheng, Litvinov & Kirschen, [Factoring the cycle aging cost of batteries participating in electricity markets](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.04567), IEEE Transactions on Power Systems, 2018.

**Planning, sizing and EV sites**

- Vermeer, Chandra Mouli & Bauer, [Optimal sizing and control of a PV-EV-BES charging system including primary frequency control and component degradation](https://doi.org/10.1109/OJIES.2022.3161091), IEEE OJIES, 2022.
- Laws et al., [study of PV-battery value under forecast uncertainty](https://docs.nrel.gov/docs/fy23osti/86194.pdf), NREL, 2023.
- Balogun et al., [EV-EcoSim](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04705), 2024.
- Brändle & Hug, [battery bidding under state-of-charge uncertainty](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12594), 2026.

**Tools and digital twins**

- Orfanoudakis et al., [EV2Gym](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01849), TU Delft, 2024.
- Zomerdijk, Palensky, AlSkaif & Vergara, [On future power system digital twins](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02568), 2024.
- Blum et al., [BOPTEST](https://doi.org/10.1080/19401493.2021.1986574), Journal of Building Performance Simulation, 2021.

**Policy, market and industry**

- [Directive (EU) 2024/1711, Art. 6a](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2024/1711/oj/eng) on flexible connection agreements.
- CEER, [Grid Connection Capacity Challenges](https://www.ceer.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/GridConnectionChallanges_CEER-paper-combined-1.pdf), June 2026.
- BlueTerra & Trinomics, [study on congestion-management uptake for the Dutch Ministry of Economic Affairs](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D33903), 2026.
- [Dutch National EMS Programme report](https://romutrechtregion.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/20260331_Rapport_Nationaal_EMS_Programma.pdf), March 2026.
- Colangelo et al. (Emerald AI), [field demonstration of a flexible AI data centre](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00909), 2025.
- Dealroom, [Grid technology and infrastructure startups in Europe](https://dealroom.co/charts/landscape-48597-grid-technology-infrastructure-startups-in-europe.html).
- Vireo and HTGF, [The European Grid Deep Dive](https://www.htgf.de/european-grid-deep-dive/), October 2026.
- Energy Systems Catapult, [Data science skills in the energy sector](https://es.catapult.org.uk/news/survey-reveals-digital-data-skills-gap-in-the-energy-sector/).
