    How to read this

    Data: CityLearn 2022 challenge dataset (phase 1), five homes, simulated with CityLearn 3.2.0 by emsbench. Hourly values, so kWh per hour equals average kW.

    Flows and charts show the five homes added together. CityLearn bills each home separately and pays nothing for exported energy, so the bill can be a little higher than the combined flows suggest.

    Prices are a fixed time-of-use tariff, 0.22 off-peak and 0.54 from 16:00 to 21:00. The currency is not stated in the dataset (assumed USD). Calendar dates are inferred: the data appear to start on 1 August; the year is unknown.

    "Cycles" means equivalent full cycles: energy discharged divided by battery capacity. A rough proxy for battery wear.
