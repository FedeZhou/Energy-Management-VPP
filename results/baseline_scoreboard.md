# Baseline scoreboard

- Dataset: `citylearn_challenge_2022_phase_1` (8759 steps)
- CityLearn 3.2.0, emsbench commit `11463fd`, run on 2026-11-07
- Absolute KPIs are computed by `emsbench.benchmark.kpis` (see its docstring for definitions). Cost is in the dataset's price unit (assumed USD, unverified).
- `cost_vs_no_battery` = total cost / total cost without battery (whole district).
- `cl_*` columns are CityLearn's own KPIs: ratios to the same buildings with PV but no battery (1.0 = no change, below 1.0 = better). At district level CityLearn reports the **unweighted mean of per-building ratios**, so `cl_cost_total` differs from `cost_vs_no_battery`: small buildings weigh as much as large ones.
